About Fatima Spagogo

Crave-worthy spicy spaghetti cooked to order in Ogun State.

Our Kitchen

Fatima Spagogo is a dedicated spicy spaghetti kitchen operating strictly on a delivery-only model. Based in Ibafo, Ogun State, we specialize in rich, savoury, pepper-infused spaghetti dishes paired with beef, chicken, fish, turkey, and prawns.

Where We Deliver

We deliver exclusively within Ibafo and Mowe in Ogun State. Delivery is complimentary across Ibafo, while delivery to Mowe starts from ₦500. All meals are prepared and dispatched between Monday and Friday, from 6:00 am to 4:00 pm.

Why We Require Payment on the Site

Every single portion is cooked fresh to order. By confirming payment directly through the website via Paystack or Stripe before cooking begins, our kitchen eliminates fake orders, minimizes food waste, and guarantees that every meal prepared has a confirmed customer waiting to receive it.

How Ordering Works

Customers select their dish, enter delivery details, and pay online. Once our server verifies payment approval, the order details are handed off to the kitchen via WhatsApp for rapid preparation and doorstep dispatch.
