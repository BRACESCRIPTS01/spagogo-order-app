Privacy Policy

How we handle customer data, payment details, and your privacy rights.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What Data We Collect and Why

When you place an order or create an account with Fatima Spagogo, we collect:

  • Full Name, Phone Number, and Delivery Address: Used strictly to prepare, fulfill, and deliver your meal.
  • Email Address: Used by the payment provider for your receipt and by us for account emails such as password resets.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Order records and account credentials are stored securely in Supabase (managed Postgres database). Access is restricted and protected by Row Level Security policies.

Payment Processing

Card numbers, PINs, bank account numbers, and tokenized credentials are handled solely by our regulated payment providers, Paystack and Stripe. This website never receives, processes, or stores your payment card details. You can review their respective privacy practices at Paystack Terms & Privacy and Stripe Privacy Policy.

Customer Support Chatbot

Messages typed into the on-site assistant are transmitted through our own server to Google Gemini solely to generate helpful answers. These chat interactions are not stored by Fatima Spagogo.

Local Storage & Cookies

We do not use tracking, advertising, or third-party analytics cookies. Your web browser’s local storage is used solely to maintain your active login session and retain your delivery details so they can be conveniently passed to the WhatsApp order confirmation hand-off.

Data Retention

Customer order details are kept as part of the business’s order records for accounting, delivery verification, and record-keeping.

Your Rights Under NDPA 2023

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to request access to your personal data, request correction of inaccurate details, or request deletion of your information.
