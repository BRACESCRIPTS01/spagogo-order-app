Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on ordering, delivery areas, payments, and accounts.

Ordering

What are your operating hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, from 6:00 am to 4:00 pm. We are closed on Saturdays and Sundays. No meals are cooked outside these hours.

Can I order multiple types of dishes at once?

The order form currently accepts one dish type per order, with your choice of quantity.

Can I cancel my order or get a refund?

Message on WhatsApp before cooking starts; refunds are handled through the payment provider.

What happens if I place an order outside opening hours?

The order form is open, but the kitchen cooks only in opening hours (Monday–Friday, 6:00 am – 4:00 pm).

Delivery

Where do you deliver?

We deliver exclusively within Ibafo and Mowe in Ogun State. No delivery is available to any other location.

What are the delivery fees?

Delivery is free across Ibafo. Delivery to Mowe starts from ₦500, with any extra distance agreed on WhatsApp after payment.

Payment

How do I pay on the site?

You can pay via Paystack (using Nigerian cards, bank transfer, or USSD) or Stripe Checkout (international cards). All prices are in naira.

Is real money charged on this website?

No. This site is a portfolio demo. Payments run in Paystack and Stripe test mode; no real money is charged and test orders are not fulfilled.

What does an approved order mean?

An order counts as placed when the payment is confirmed. Once approved, the paid order reference is sent to the kitchen on WhatsApp.

Account & Loyalty

Do I need an account to order?

Accounts are optional; guest ordering works smoothly. Creating an account gives you email confirmations, password reset, and access to your order history.

How do loyalty points work?

You earn 1 point per ₦100 on approved orders, shown on the “My orders” page. Please note that points cannot be redeemed yet.

How do I reset my password?

Click “Log in / Sign up” on the order page, select “Forgot your password?”, enter your registered email address, and follow the link sent to your inbox.

How can I reach the kitchen?

You can reach us via WhatsApp or phone at +234 705 507 6189 during opening hours.

Still unsure? Use the chat bubble on the order page.
