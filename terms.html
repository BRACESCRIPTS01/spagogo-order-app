Terms of Service

Conditions of service, ordering policies, and demo limitations.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

1. Demo Build Notice

While this site runs in test mode, payments are not real and test orders are not fulfilled. This platform serves as a working demonstration for Fatima Spagogo kitchen.

2. Ordering Hours & Delivery Areas

Orders are prepared and dispatched strictly between Monday and Friday, from 6:00 am to 4:00 pm. The kitchen is closed on Saturdays and Sundays. Delivery is offered exclusively within Ibafo (free delivery) and Mowe (from ₦500, with any additional distance surcharge agreed on WhatsApp after payment).

3. Pricing & Order Formation

All prices on the website are listed in Nigerian Naira (₦). An order counts as placed once payment is confirmed by our payment gateway. Unconfirmed or unpaid orders are neither received nor prepared by the kitchen.

4. Cancellations & Refunds

To cancel an order, you must message us on WhatsApp before cooking starts. Approved refunds are processed directly through the payment provider (Paystack or Stripe).

5. Loyalty Points

Registered customers earn 1 loyalty point per ₦100 spent on approved orders, visible in their account history. Points cannot be redeemed yet.

6. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Fatima Spagogo accepts no liability for any indirect, incidental, or consequential losses arising from the use of this website or demonstration service.

7. Governing Law

These terms and any operational disputes are governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
