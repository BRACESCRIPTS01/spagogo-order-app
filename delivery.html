Delivery & Hours

Freshly cooked spicy spaghetti delivered hot across Ibafo and Mowe.

Operating Hours

Day Kitchen Hours
Monday – Friday 6:00 am – 4:00 pm
Saturday – Sunday Closed

Orders Outside Operating Hours

The online order form remains open at all times, but the kitchen cooks and delivers only during opening hours (Monday–Friday, 6:00 am – 4:00 pm). No orders are cooked outside these hours.

Delivery Areas & Fees

Zone Delivery Fee Details
Ibafo Free Complimentary delivery across the Ibafo axis.
Mowe From ₦500 ₦500 base fee added at checkout; extra distance agreed on WhatsApp.

Delivery is restricted strictly to Ibafo and Mowe in Ogun State. For distant addresses in Mowe, any additional rider charge beyond the ₦500 minimum is settled directly on WhatsApp after payment.

How It Works in 4 Steps

  1. Choose: Select your meal on the order form and specify your delivery address and zone.
  2. Pay on the site: Complete payment securely using Paystack (card, bank transfer, USSD) or Stripe Checkout (international cards). All prices are in naira.
  3. Payment confirmed: Your transaction is verified automatically by the server. An order counts as placed once payment is confirmed.
  4. Kitchen gets the order on WhatsApp: Tap the WhatsApp button to hand off your paid order reference and delivery address to the kitchen for cooking and dispatch.

Payment Methods & Demo Notice

We support payments via Paystack (Nigerian debit and credit cards, bank transfer, and USSD) and Stripe Checkout (international cards). On this portfolio demo, both payment gateways operate in test mode. No real money is charged and test orders are not fulfilled.
